Let's Clear It Up

Straight answers to what homeowners ask us most often.

Still unsure? Just call: 615-561-0472
Q01 Are you licensed and insured?

Yes, Triple Oak Services is fully licensed and insured for your protection on every job, big or small.

Q02 Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. We provide free, no-obligation estimates for every service, just give us a call at 615-561-0472.

Q03 How quickly can you respond to storm damage?

We prioritize storm damage and emergency calls and do our best to get a crew out the same day whenever possible.

Q04 Will you clean up and haul away all the debris?

Absolutely. Every job includes full clean-up, we haul away branches, logs, and debris so your property looks better than before we arrived.

Q05 Do you grind stumps below ground level?

Yes, we grind stumps below grade so you can landscape, plant, or seed right over the area when we're done.

Q06 What areas do you serve?

We proudly serve Nashville, TN and the surrounding area. Call us to confirm service in your neighborhood.
